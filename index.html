STAR TREKVECTOR

STRATEGIC OPERATIONS SIMULATOR — THE 1982 SEGA CABINET

The Klingons are invading Federation space, sector by sector. From the Enterprise bridge: burn them down with phasers, finish heavy cruisers with photon torpedoes, and dock with starbases to repair shields and refill your energy and torpedo bays.

As close to the 1982 Sega coin-op as vector lines allow. Pick your starting sector like a seasoned captain — a deeper start earns bonus shields. But out here the starbase is in sector from the first second and the Klingons shoot at it: lose it and there's no resupply until the next sector. And from Sector 3, the Nomad probe comes hunting — phasers won't scratch it. Photons only.

PHASERS

Fast and unlimited in number — but every shot draws warp energy. Watch the gauge.

PHOTONS

Devastating area blasts that wreck anything nearby. Limited stock — press T or the PHOTON button.

STARBASE

When one is in sector, steer your sights onto it and hold to dock: full shields, energy and torpedoes.

1 · PROTECT

The starbase is under fire from the off. Shoot down the green bolts aimed at it, or kill their gunners — five hits and it's gone.

2 · NOMAD

From Sector 3 the Nomad probe stalks the sector, beelining for the starbase — or for you. Phasers spark off it; only a photon blast stops it. 1,500 points.

3 · DOCK

Hold your sights on the starbase to dock: full shields, energy and torpedoes. Warping on no longer tops up your photons — the base is the only resupply.

BATTLEZONEVECTOR

THE 1980 ATARI CABINET — FIRST-PERSON ARMOUR

You are the last tank on a wireframe plain under a volcano sky. Enemy armour is rolling in from every point of the compass — your periscope sight and the radar at the top of the screen are all that stand between you and the scrapyard.

1 · RADAR

Red blips are enemy tanks closing in; the wedge is you. Swing your hull around and keep them in front of your gun.

2 · ARMOUR

Tanks die to one shell. White super tanks are faster, shoot quicker, and take two. The UFO is a 5,000-point gift — if you can hit it.

3 · SURVIVE

One enemy shell costs you a tank, and you have three. Blocks and pyramids stop shells — use them as cover. Clear the wave quota to advance.
